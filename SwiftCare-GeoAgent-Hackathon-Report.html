SwiftCare GeoAgent01 · Overview
01Executive summary

Ambulance routes are planned once.
The road keeps changing.

SwiftCare GeoAgent watches an ambulance's GPS trail against the corridor it was sent on. When the vehicle drifts, slows, or runs into a reported incident, the system works out what that means for arrival time, compares alternatives, and puts a clear recommendation in front of a control-room operator — who approves or rejects it. The software never changes a mission on its own.

The problem

Drift

Off the planned road

A missed turn or forced detour puts the ambulance on a road the plan never considered. Dispatch finds out only when the crew calls in — or the ETA quietly slips.

Delay

Congestion & incidents

A crash or closure can add several minutes. The information often exists somewhere — but it isn't tied to the vehicle and route it affects.

Escalation

Backup decided too late

Whether a closer unit should also go is a judgement call made under pressure, usually without a side-by-side comparison of arrival times.

For cardiac arrest, stroke and major trauma, minutes of delay are widely recognised as affecting outcomes. Our goal is narrow and testable: notice a problem on the road as soon as the data shows it, explain it, and give a person a safe, auditable choice.

What makes it different

Rules decide, AI explains

The language model can only read data. If it disagrees with the rules, the decision is flagged AI_RECOMMENDATION_CONFLICT — never overridden.

Honest about uncertainty

Every prediction factor is tagged observed, derived, inferred or unknown. Missing data raises an alert — never a guess.

Approvals that can't go stale

Executing a reroute re-checks that the approved route is unchanged; otherwise it returns HTTP 409 and asks for fresh analysis.

Runs on zero paid services

OSRM routing, free-only AI model selection, and graceful fallbacks on every external dependency.

SwiftCare GeoAgent02 · Use Case 2 fit
02Problem statement fit

Built for Use Case 2, point by point

The scenario: an ambulance with a 10-minute ETA deviates because of an accident and congestion. The control room must answer five questions fast. SwiftCare answers each one live, on one screen.

Control-room questionHow SwiftCare answers it
Q1 Has the ambulance deviated?Distance + heading bands at 50 / 100 / 250 m, confirmed over 3 GPS fixes so jitter doesn't trigger false alarms.
Q2 What caused it?Correlates nearby incidents, traffic level and corridor status into reason codes — e.g. CRITICAL_INCIDENT, HEAVY_TRAFFIC.
Q3 How much delay?Predicted vs. baseline ETA and delay in minutes, each factor labelled observed, derived, inferred or unknown.
Q4 Best alternative route?Road-following OSRM alternatives scored on ETA + traffic + incidents; must save ≥ 2 min to be recommended.
Q5 Send another ambulance?CONSIDER_BACKUP when a unit within 10 km is ≥ 5 min faster on a high-priority call. The operator decides.

05System architecture

Three tiers, deployed and live

A Next.js frontend on Vercel, an Express + MongoDB backend on Render, and real-time updates over Socket.IO.

FIG 2Teal services are live in the deployment; grey ones are supported in code and switch on with an API key.

Key design decisions

  • Same-origin proxy. The browser never talks to the backend directly, so the session cookie stays HttpOnly and the backend address stays private.
  • Rules in plain code. Every outcome traces to a line in decision.rules.js and a reason code.
  • MongoDB + GeoJSON. 2dsphere indexes answer “what is near this point” without a separate GIS database.
  • Background prediction. GPS writes return instantly; forecasts follow over sockets.

SwiftCare GeoAgent07 · Core technology
07Core technology

Detecting drift without crying wolf

Phone GPS often jumps 20–60 m near flyovers. SwiftCare uses distance and heading, plus a stability window, so real deviations are caught and noise is ignored.

FIG 4All thresholds are environment-tunable. A 30-second cooldown stops repeated reroute suggestions.

08GeoAgent AI

An AI assistant that can only read

GeoAgent writes a plain-language situation summary and a suggested action. It gathers facts through 12 read-only tools — none of them can write — and its opinion never changes the rule outcome.

FIG 5A model is re-checked against the free catalogue right before every call, so a paid model is never used by accident.

No guessing

Every fact is observed or derived; anything the agent couldn't fetch stays UNKNOWN.

Maths in code

A route is “materially better” only if it saves ≥ 2 min — computed in code, not by the model.

Low confidence flagged

Answers below 0.5 confidence are marked for operator review.

Delay prediction you can audit

After every GPS point, a background job updates predicted ETA, delay, route risk (low → critical) and reroute urgency (none → immediate). Each input factor carries a label, so the operator always knows how solid the number is:

Observed

Measured directly, e.g. GPS speed

Derived

Calculated from measurements

Inferred

Estimated, e.g. traffic impact

Unknown

Missing — never filled with a guess

SwiftCare GeoAgent09 · Decisions
09Decision engine

Recommend, approve, execute —
three separate steps

A decision is a stored record: the recommended action, the reasons, a snapshot of every input, and who did what to it and when.

FIG 6Only control-room and admin roles can approve, reject or execute. Re-analysing an unchanged situation within 30 s returns the existing decision — no duplicates. Thresholds are prototype policy values, not yet medically validated.

The rules, in order

#ConditionOutcome
1Emergency or vehicle data missing, or vehicle not operationalAlert  INSUFFICIENT_DATA
2Deviated · heavy traffic · critical incident on corridor · V2X corridor blockedMarks needs reroute with reason codes
3Score each alternative: ETA + traffic weight + incident weightBest route that saves ≥ 2 min wins
4Needs reroute and a viable alternative existsReroute
5Needs reroute but no viable alternativeAlert  no invented route
6High-priority call, ETA > 15 min or delay > 8 min, and a backup ≥ 5 min faster within 10 kmConsider backup
7None of the aboveContinue
8GeoAgent suggests something differentAdds AI_RECOMMENDATION_CONFLICT; result unchanged

What “execute” actually does

Reroute

Activates the approved route — only if it is unchanged since approval. Otherwise HTTP 409: re-analyse and approve again.

Consider backup

Recorded as a recommendation. It never dispatches a second vehicle automatically.

Alert

Pushes a live event to the control room. Continue / no-action outcomes are logged for audit.

SwiftCare GeoAgent10 · Trust & proof
10Security & safeguards

Built to be trusted in a control room

Security

  • bcrypt passwords; JWT sessions in an HttpOnly, Secure cookie.
  • New accounts stay pending until an admin approves.
  • Every data route checks role on the server; crews can only touch their own vehicle.
  • Socket connections authenticated; room joins re-checked per role.
  • Rate limits: login 30 / 15 min · AI 30 / min · writes 120 / min.
  • Gitleaks scans every commit and the full history in CI.

  • Graceful fallback on every external dependency: routing, snapping, traffic, AI and Python.

Conclusion

SwiftCare GeoAgent shows that continuous route monitoring and decision support for ambulances can be built entirely from open components — with AI kept in an explanatory role and a person in charge of every change. Tracking, deviation, prediction and the rule engine are implemented, deployed and tested. Switching on live traffic data and real device GPS takes it from a strong prototype to a usable control-room tool.
