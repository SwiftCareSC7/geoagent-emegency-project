02 The problem

The route is planned once. The road keeps changing.

After dispatch, nobody has time to watch every GPS trace. Problems are visible in the data minutes before anyone acts on them.

Drift

Off the planned road

A missed turn or forced detour. The dispatcher finds out when the crew calls in, or when the ETA quietly slips.

Delay

Congestion and incidents

A crash ahead can add minutes. The report exists somewhere, but it is not linked to the vehicle it affects.

Escalation

Backup decided late

Should a closer unit also go? That call is made under pressure, without arrival times side by side.

SayFor cardiac arrest, stroke and trauma, minutes matter. We are not claiming a clinical result; we built the part that notices the problem early and makes the next decision easy and safe.
SwiftCare GeoAgent
03 Our solution

A loop that watches every mission

Each GPS point goes through the same six steps. The software recommends; a control-room operator decides.

Position

GPS point stored and snapped to the planned road.

Corridor

Distance and heading give on-route, warning, deviated or critical.

Forecast

ETA and delay updated, each input labelled observed, derived, inferred or unknown.

Rules

Fixed rules pick continue, reroute, consider backup or alert, with reason codes.

Explain

GeoAgent AI reads the facts and explains them. It cannot change the result.

Approve

Operator approves or rejects. Stale approvals are refused.

SayThe AI is in the passenger seat. The rules are readable code, and a person signs off every change to a mission.

05 Website walkthrough

Home, sign in and registration

/ · /login · /register — everyone

Home, sign in and registration

What you see

  • Headline “Every second matters. We get you there faster.” and three cards: real-time monitoring, delay prediction, smart rerouting.
  • Personnel Sign In, View Driver Dashboard, a Help guide for the roles, and a contact section.
  • Registration asks for a role (control room, driver or paramedic) and workspaces. Admin can never be requested.

How it works

  • New accounts are Pending until an admin approves; pending, suspended or rejected users get a clear message.
  • Login and registration are rate-limited (30 tries per 15 minutes).
SAYIn an emergency system, who can approve a reroute matters. Access is earned, not self-assigned.
what happens on sign-in

From password to workspace

Steps

  • The browser posts to /api/auth/login on our own site.
  • The website forwards it to the Express backend, which checks the bcrypt password hash and the account status.
  • The backend signs a session token; it is stored as an HttpOnly cookie (7 days) that page scripts cannot read.
  • The user lands on their role’s screen: admin → /admin, dispatcher → /control-room, driver → /driver/dashboard.
  • If the backend is unreachable, production login fails closed instead of using any local fallback.

Control Room

/control-room — dispatchers, admin

Control Room

What you see

  • Five live counters: Active Calls, Critical Calls, Deployed Units, Fleet Ready, Road Hazards.
  • A Leaflet map of Bengaluru with every ambulance, emergency, incident and route corridor.
  • A stream badge: Control stream live (sockets), Polling live API (fallback) or Simulated.
  • The mission assessment panel: deviation, ETA, the recommended action and its reasons.
  • Side panels: corridor clearance (simulated connected vehicles), fleet units, road incidents.
  • Buttons: Refresh Live Data, Broadcast Alert, Audit History, Push-to-Talk, Task Routing, Manual Override, Assign Unit, What-If Simulator.
under the hood

How it works

Data and live updates

  • First load is fetched on the server; then Socket.IO pushes positions, incidents, ETA, predictions and decisions as they happen. If the socket drops, it polls.
  • Operators join the control-room room automatically, so they see every mission; drivers never do.
  • Audit History reads the stored decisions for the selected emergency.
  • Honest notes: Broadcast Alert and Push-to-Talk only show a confirmation (no message or audio is sent). Approve, Override, Assign and Task Routing work on the backend but are not yet wired through on the live site.
SAYOne screen tells a dispatcher where every unit is, which calls are critical and what is in the way, and it updates without a refresh.

under the hood

How it works

Routing and safety

Three services, one data path

SayEverything runs on free tiers. Every external dependency has a fallback: Google to OSRM, AI to rules, Python to JavaScript.
SwiftCare GeoAgent
13 GeoAgent AI

An assistant that can only read

  • 12 read-only tools: emergency, vehicle, trail, route, alternatives, traffic, prediction, incidents, decision history, situation, nearby units, green-wave status.
  • Bounded: at most 6 rounds, 3 calls per round, 8 calls in total, 8 s per tool. Repeated calls are de-duplicated.
  • Free models only: OpenRouter, then OpenCode. Each model is checked against the live free catalog right before it is called.
  • Honest: anything it could not fetch stays "unknown"; confidence below 0.5 is flagged for review.
  • No model? Still works: a rule-based answer marked fallback: true.
SayIf the AI disagrees with the rules, the decision records an AI-conflict reason code for the operator. It never flips the outcome. Today no model key is configured live, so the demo shows the fallback.

14 Routing, ETA and telemetry

From a GPS point to a deviation state

Routing

Real roads

OSRM (OpenStreetMap) live; Google when a key is set. Straight-line or broken geometry is rejected.

ETA

Forecast with evidence

Predicted ETA, delay and risk after each point, every factor labelled observed, derived, inferred or unknown.

Telemetry

Fast ingestion

Points are saved and broadcast at once; prediction runs in the background. Demo telemetry is seeded and played back.

SayPhone GPS jumps 20 to 60 metres near flyovers, so we wait for three agreeing fixes. Above 250 metres we report immediately. Traffic in the demo is mock data, and we label it that way.

16 Safety and responsible operation

Designed so a mistake is hard to make

Access

Earned, not self-assigned

New accounts wait for admin approval. Every endpoint checks role on the server; crews see only their own vehicle, over HTTP and sockets. 86/86 access checks pass.

AI boundary

Explains, never acts

Read-only tools, bounded loop, free models only, disagreement recorded. The system works with no AI at all.

Actions

Nothing changes by itself

Approval required; stale approvals refused; backup units are recommended, never auto-dispatched; duplicate decisions within 30 s are merged.

Secrets

Out of the code

Gitleaks scans every push and the full history. Session cookie is HttpOnly. Database open only to an IP allow list.

Data

No real patients

Demo data only. Telemetry expires after 90 days. Destructive resets are blocked against production.

Honesty

Simulated means labelled

Traffic is mock, the signal corridor and voice are simulated, and clearance sessions carry an isSimulated flag.

Prototype boundaries

23 Judge Q&A

Questions we expect (1/2)

  • What if the AI is wrong? It cannot act. Fixed rules make the recommendation, the AI only explains, and a person approves. If it disagrees with the rules, that is recorded as a reason code. With no AI at all, the rules still run.
  • How do you avoid false alarms from bad GPS? Three distance bands (50 / 100 / 250 m) plus heading, and three consistent fixes before we call a deviation. Above 250 m we report at once.
  • Is any of this real? The platform is deployed and works end to end: accounts, database, routing on real roads, live updates, the rules. Missions are seeded demo data; traffic, V2X and vitals are simulated, and we label them.
  • Why not just use Google Maps? Google gives a route. We watch the mission against that route, notice when it goes wrong, compare recovery options and put a reasoned choice in front of the control room.
  • Why do some live buttons not work? The backend for approval, override and assign is built and tested. The website is missing the small routes that pass those calls through. It is our first fix; the Emergency Lab shows the flow meanwhile.

24 Judge Q&A

Questions we expect (2/2)

  • How does it scale? State lives in MongoDB, each screen subscribes only to the rooms it needs, and the backend is a normal Node service. The free hosting tier is today’s limit, not the design.
  • What about security and privacy? Admin-approved accounts, server-side role and vehicle-ownership checks on HTTP and sockets (86/86 tests pass), HttpOnly sessions, secret scanning in CI, the database open only to an IP allow list. No real patient data.
  • Which AI model do you use? Free models only, chosen at call time from OpenRouter or OpenCode, with a bounded tool loop. No paid key is needed, and the demo runs on the rule-based fallback.
  • How did you test it? CI runs secret scanning, typecheck, build, backend suites against MongoDB and a Docker build, and it passed on our final commit. Access control alone has 86 automated checks.
  • What is next? Wire the remaining buttons, upload driver GPS, live traffic and a model key, then a pilot on recorded missions to tune the thresholds with practitioners.
